## 1. Hero / Overview Section

**Heading:** Livelihood

**Sub-heading (tagline):**
Strengthening rural incomes through farming, enterprise, and community-driven innovation.

**Overview paragraph:**
KSLI's Livelihood vertical works at the intersection of agriculture, entrepreneurship, and rural development — helping farming communities build resilient, diversified income streams. Our approach spans three interconnected pathways: **Farm-Based Livelihood** (agriculture, animal husbandry, integrated farming systems), **Off-Farm Livelihood** (agro-processing, rural manufacturing, value-addition enterprises), and **Non-Farm Livelihood** (rural services, trade, micro-enterprises). Through applied research, farmer producer organizations, capacity-building programs, and industry partnerships, we work directly with thousands of farmers across Tamil Nadu to improve productivity, incomes, and long-term climate resilience.

*(Optional secondary line for visual balance under the overview:)*
From dairy entrepreneurship to sugarcane farmer networks, KSLI's livelihood initiatives are designed to be practical, scalable, and rooted in real field partnerships.

## 3. Research & Field Innovation

**Heading:** Research Driving Livelihood Impact

**Intro line:**
Our livelihood work is grounded in applied, field-tested research — not just policy on paper.

**Content blocks:**

- **Sustainable Agriculture — Fertilizer Optimization (N-Balancing) in Sugarcane**
  An integrated research and extension program supporting over 5,000 farmers supplying to Sakthi Sugars, combining scientific nitrogen-balancing with on-ground farmer capacity building.

- **Climate Smart Dairy**
  Focused on productivity enhancement through entrepreneurship and farmer training, alongside a digital, evidence-based extension model for smallholder dairy productivity.

- **Dutch Fund for Climate Action (DFCD)**
  A ~₹5 crore climate adaptation grant implemented in partnership with WWF India, focused on scalable, nature-based livelihood solutions.

**Research partners:** University of Hohenheim, Environmental Defense Fund, NITARA, Arulagam, WWF, DFCD

## 4. Projects

**Heading:** Livelihood Projects

**Intro line:**
From farmer producer organizations to entrepreneurship programs, KSLI runs livelihood projects that translate research into real income impact.

**Core Livelihood Projects:**
- **Climate Smart Dairy Entrepreneurship Program** — building farmer-led dairy enterprises with climate-resilient practices
- **Pariyur Farmer Producer Organization** — a farmer-owned collective strengthening market access and bargaining power
- **Farmer 360 Initiative** — a holistic support model addressing the full spectrum of farmer needs
- **Young Farmers Forum** — engaging the next generation of farmers in modern, sustainable agriculture

**CSR-Funded Livelihood Projects:**
- **Uzhavan Foundation, Manaparai**
- **Armour Steel Buildings, Salem**

**Key institutional partners:** StartupTN, Aavin, Environmental Defense Fund, ABT Foods, Pariyur

**Centres of Excellence anchoring these projects:**
- CoE – Dairy
- CoE – Sugarcane
- CoE – Social Development

*(Backed by industry partners including Sakthi Sugars and Ashok Leyland)*
